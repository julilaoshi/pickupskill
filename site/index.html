Pickupskill / 文件整理 skill

Your files are not missing. Your folder is just unusable.

A cautious organizing skill that scans first, protects project packages, keeps uncertain files in review, and never deletes by default.

Read Skill Star Repo

What It Does

Sort loose files first

Root-level screenshots, PDFs, videos, notes, and exports are treated as the newest cleanup targets.

Protect project packages

Source files, previews, subtitles, documents, and related assets stay together when they clearly belong together.

Use review buckets

Uncertain items are not forced into fake categories. They stay visible for a later human decision.

Chinese Pitch

文件一多，桌面像被作业本淹了？它专门干这个：帮你把乱七八糟的文件先分门别类。 它不是乱收拾，是先看清楚：哪些是图片、哪些是视频、哪些是文档、哪些像一个项目包。 最重要的是：它不乱删东西。看不懂的，不硬塞；有风险的，先放到待判定。 它像一个特别听话的整理委员，先处理散落在外面的新文件；已经成套的东西，它不会拆散。 一句话：这个 Skill 不是帮你变勤快，它是让你不用那么勤快，也能把文件整理得像个正常人。
